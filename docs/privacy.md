# Privacy policy

<p class="updated">Last updated: 26 October 2026</p>

Simple Home TV is a home screen app for Android TV, published as open source at
[github.com/xiaodoudou/simple-home-tv](https://github.com/xiaodoudou/simple-home-tv). It has no ads, no analytics, no
account of its own and no server of its own. Nothing about you is sent to the developer.

## What stays on the TV

Everything the app keeps is stored on the TV, in the app's private storage: your settings, which apps are on the
home and in what order, and the aerial videos it downloaded.
Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What the app connects to

- **Apple's servers**, to download the aerial videos shown in the background and on the screensaver.
- **The apps on your TV**, through Android's TV content provider, to show what they publish (Continue watching,
  channels). The pictures in those rows come from the addresses those apps give.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information, from children or anyone else.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions go to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/xiaodoudou/simple-home-tv/issues).
